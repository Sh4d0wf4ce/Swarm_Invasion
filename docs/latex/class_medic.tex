\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Support hero with teleport, healing orb, barrier wall, and drone ultimate. 

Fires healing needles on left click, launches directional orbs on right click, teleports on Shift with a fade animation, places arc barriers on E, and commands a healing drone ultimate on Q.
