Detailed Description

Heavy shotgun hero with charge dash, repulsor cone, and black hole.

Uses limited shotgun ammo with reload, a directional charge on Shift, a frontal repulsor blast on right click, black hole placement on E, and rapid-fire recoil shooting during the ultimate.
